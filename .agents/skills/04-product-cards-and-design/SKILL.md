# Product Card UX: 77569.mp4
- Hierarchy: one priority message/action with accurate title, price, category and status.
- Spacing: consistent gaps and aligned copy, avoid overly dense cards.
- Color: neutrals, secondary shades and one accent; support legibility and contrast.
- Depth: light from above, restraint with shadows, no low contrast.
- Corners: align inner/outer radii; watch nested clipping.
- Motion: animate feedback without delaying task or misleading user.
Apply where appropriate; do not convert security/finance admin tables into decorative marketing cards. Never hide actual fees, payment chain, vesting, refunds or jurisdictional restrictions.
